[SUMMARY]

Pirates are far more than thieves of the sea. They are the Free Captains, sailors who rejected the rule of kings to chase the mysteries hidden beyond the horizon. Bound by an ancient code of loyalty, courage, and freedom, they sail dangerous waters in search of forgotten ports, legendary treasures, and secrets the world has long buried.

Among all pirate legends, none is more feared than Captain Vane and his ghostly vessel, The Black Sails. Appearing without warning beneath pitch-black sails, the ship leaves no wake and carries a silent crew whose true nature remains unknown. Whether they are guardians, monsters, or something far older, no one can say. Sailors know only one thing: when The Black Sails is seen upon the horizon, the sea is about to remember a debt.

[THE AGE BEFORE THE TIDE]

Long before kingdoms measured their borders, the sea belonged to no crown.

Sailors speak of an age when the ocean was calm beyond reason. Storms were rare, monsters were little more than oversized fish, and voyages could last years without danger. Trade flourished, empires expanded, and rulers believed the sea had finally been conquered.

They were wrong.

The sea was never conquered.

It was simply... waiting.

No one knows what changed first. Some claim a star fell beyond the horizon. Others whisper that something sleeping beneath the deepest trench stirred for the first time in countless centuries.

Within a single generation, the oceans transformed.

Currents shifted overnight.

Entire islands disappeared beneath impossible fog.

Ships returned months after they had sunk.

Compasses began pointing toward places that did not exist.

That was the day sailors stopped calling themselves merchants.

That was the day the first pirates were born.

---

[THE FIRST FREE CAPTAINS]

Unlike common criminals, the earliest pirates were explorers who refused to serve kings.

Every kingdom demanded taxes.

Every empire claimed ownership of the sea.

The Free Captains answered with one simple belief:

"No one owns the horizon."

They painted over royal colours.

They tore down naval banners.

Every ship sailed beneath its own symbol.

Some sought riches.

Others hunted monsters.

A handful searched for places the maps refused to acknowledge.

Many died.

The survivors became legends.

Their crews were rarely loyal to coin alone.

A captain earned followers through courage, fairness, and the promise of seeing what no ordinary sailor ever would.

---

[THE PIRATE CODE]

Contrary to popular belief, pirates are not lawless.

They simply obey different laws.

The oldest crews follow traditions older than any kingdom.

Among them are several sacred rules:

• Never abandon a crewmate while breath remains.

• Share treasure according to effort, not rank.

• The sea judges captains before kings ever can.

• Never draw steel aboard a peaceful ship without cause.

• The dead are committed to the sea with respect.

Breaking these traditions is believed to anger the ocean itself.

Whether that is superstition or truth remains unknown.

---

[THE BLACK FLAGS]

Not every pirate sails beneath black.

Colours carry meaning.

Black banners announce that mercy has ended.

Crimson sails belong to captains who promise bloodshed.

White banners often hide smugglers.

Deep blue belongs to navigators of dangerous waters.

Yet one banner is feared above all others.

Solid black.

No emblem.

No markings.

Only darkness.

Sailors call these...

The Black Sails.

No crew willingly copies them.

Some believe the colour itself is cursed.

Others claim no ordinary cloth remains that black beneath years of sun and salt.

---

[THE BLACK SAILS]

Few can agree whether The Black Sails is a ship...

...or something pretending to be one.

Witnesses describe different vessels.

Some remember a towering warship.

Others swear it resembles an ancient brig.

A handful insist it changes with every sighting.

Only three details never change.

Pitch-black sails.

Silent crew.

No lanterns.

It leaves no wake.

Its cannons are never heard.

Yet ports disappear after its passing.

Some say the ship only appears when the sea wishes to collect an unpaid debt.

Others insist it hunts those who steal more than treasure.

No one has successfully followed it.

No one knows where it anchors.

---

[CAPTAIN VANE]

No pirate inspires more arguments than Captain Vane.

To some, he was the greatest pirate ever to sail.

To others, he never existed at all.

Stories disagree on nearly everything.

His height.

His face.

His voice.

Even his age.

The only consistent truth is this:

Whenever The Black Sails appears...

Captain Vane stands at its helm.

He is always calm.

Always watching.

Never shouting orders.

His crew obeys without command.

No sailor remembers seeing him eat.

Few remember hearing him speak.

Those who claim to have met him often recall only one sentence.

"The sea remembers."

Then nothing more.

---

[THE CREW WITHOUT NAMES]

The crew of The Black Sails have become legends in their own right.

No one knows their names.

No one remembers their faces.

Many swear the crew changes each time the ship appears.

Others insist it is always the same sailors.

They move with unsettling precision.

They never laugh.

Never argue.

Never sing.

Some witnesses report seeing familiar faces among them.

A father lost at sea.

A missing sister.

An old captain long buried.

When approached...

those faces are never quite the same.

---

[THE HIDDEN PORTS]

Every pirate dreams of discovering a Hidden Port.

These secret harbours are said to exist beyond ordinary maps.

Some appear only during certain tides.

Others only beneath moonless skies.

Within them lie:

Shipwrights capable of repairing impossible damage.

Merchants who trade in forgotten relics.

Cartographers whose maps redraw themselves.

Taverns where stories are worth more than gold.

Finding one is considered the greatest achievement of any captain.

Leaving one alive is even rarer.

---

[THE SEA'S TREASURES]

Gold is plentiful.

Legends are not.

Experienced pirates rarely chase ordinary riches.

Instead they seek objects whispered about across every tavern.

Coins that always return.

Compasses pointing toward unanswered questions.

Lanterns that reveal hidden shores.

Keys with no known locks.

Pieces of ancient maps missing entire oceans.

Such treasures are rarely valuable because of what they are.

They are valuable because of where they eventually lead.

---

[THE SEA MONSTERS]

Not every horror beneath the waves possesses teeth.

Some resemble islands until ships anchor upon them.

Others imitate drifting wreckage.

There are creatures that swim beneath entire fleets, never surfacing.

Whirlpools sometimes have eyes.

Fog occasionally breathes.

Veteran pirates learn one lesson quickly.

If the sea behaves strangely...

leave.

Curiosity has drowned more captains than cannon fire.

---

[THE TAVERNS OF PIRATES]

Every great pirate tale begins in a tavern.

Not because sailors enjoy drinking.

Because information travels faster than ships.

Captains recruit crews.

Navigators sell routes.

Treasure hunters compare maps.

Bounty hunters ask dangerous questions.

Every port has one tavern where truths are exchanged.

Finding it is easy.

Knowing who to trust is impossible.

---

[THE SONGS OF THE SEA]

Pirates believe every voyage deserves a song.

Some celebrate victories.

Others mourn fallen crews.

Many are warnings disguised as entertainment.

Experienced sailors pay close attention whenever an old deckhand refuses to sing a particular verse.

It usually means the ending happened to someone they knew.

A pirate may forget where treasure lies.

He will never forget the song that carried him home.

---

[THE LEGACY OF PIRATES]

Most kingdoms describe pirates as thieves.

Most sailors describe them as survivors.

Most children describe them as heroes.

The truth lies somewhere between.

Pirates are the people willing to sail where maps end.

They challenge monsters instead of fleeing them.

They uncover forgotten places.

They recover things the world was never meant to find.

Sometimes they become legends.

Sometimes warnings.

But every generation produces another captain who looks toward the endless horizon and believes there is still one more mystery waiting beyond it.

And somewhere, far beyond every known sea, where fog swallows the stars and the waves move without wind...

The Black Sails still waits.

Captain Vane still watches.

And the sea...

still remembers.
